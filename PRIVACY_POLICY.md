# Privacy Policy for Lumina

**Last Updated:** September 18, 2026  
**Effective Date:** September 18, 2026  

This Privacy Policy describes how **Lumina** ("Lumina", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects personal information when you access our website, applications, automated news generation and distribution services, or connect your account through the TikTok Developer Platform (the "Service").

We respect your privacy and are committed to handling your data transparently and in compliance with global privacy regulations, including the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Information You Provide via TikTok Authentication
When you authenticate with Lumina using TikTok Login or authorize Lumina to interact with your TikTok account, we may receive information permitted by your authorization scopes:
- **TikTok User Identifier:** Open ID and/or Union ID (unique identifiers assigned by TikTok).
- **Public Profile Information:** Display name and profile avatar image.
- **Publishing & Media Permissions:** Temporary OAuth access tokens enabling video upload and publication to your TikTok account pursuant to the scopes you explicitly granted.

### B. News & Content Data
- Content and metadata (e.g., video titles, descriptions, hashtags, timestamps) created, curated, or scheduled through Lumina for publication on TikTok.

### C. Technical & Usage Data
- Log files, IP addresses, browser and device specifications, access dates and times, and API response metrics collected automatically to maintain system security and service availability.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect strictly to operate, maintain, and deliver the Service:
- **Authentication:** To verify your identity and manage your connected TikTok session.
- **Content Delivery & Publishing:** To schedule, render, and publish news videos and posts to your authorized TikTok account.
- **Service Integrity & Security:** To monitor API rate limits, prevent abuse, detect unauthorized access, and ensure server stability.
- **Customer Support:** To respond to technical inquiries, feedback, and support requests.

We do **NOT** use your personal information or TikTok data for advertising, profiling, or cross-site tracking.

---

## 3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We treat your personal information with strict confidentiality:
- **TikTok / ByteDance:** Data exchanged via the TikTok API is processed in accordance with [TikTok's Privacy Policy](https://www.tiktok.com/legal/privacy-policy) and TikTok Developer Policies.
- **No Sale or Rent of Data:** We do **never sell, rent, monetize, or trade** your personal information or TikTok account data to any third party or data broker.
- **Legal Requirements:** We may disclose data only if required to do so by applicable law, regulation, subpoena, or governmental request.

---

## 4. Data Storage & Security

- **Encryption:** All data in transit is encrypted using industry-standard TLS (Transport Layer Security / HTTPS).
- **Token Security:** TikTok access tokens and refresh tokens are stored securely with restricted access controls and encryption at rest.
- **Minimal Retention:** We retain TikTok authentication tokens only for the duration needed to fulfill authorized publishing tasks. Temporary processing files are routinely purged.

---

## 5. User Data Deletion & Revocation (TikTok Users)

You have full control over your data and can revoke permissions or request complete deletion at any time:

### How to Revoke Access via TikTok:
1. Open the **TikTok** mobile application.
2. Tap **Profile** in the bottom-right corner.
3. Tap the **Menu** (three lines icon) in the top-right corner > **Settings and privacy**.
4. Tap **Security and permissions** > **Manage app permissions**.
5. Select **Lumina** and tap **Remove access**.

Once access is revoked, Lumina can no longer access your TikTok account or publish on your behalf.

### How to Request Complete Data Deletion:
You can request permanent deletion of any data associated with your TikTok identifier stored in Lumina's systems by contacting us:
- **Email:** lumina@warclamerz.mfg
- **Subject:** Data Deletion Request - Lumina TikTok
- **Details:** Please provide your TikTok handle or Open ID. We will verify your request and permanently delete your stored records within 30 days of receipt, confirming deletion via email.

## 6. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have the right to:
- **Access:** Request copies of your personal data held by Lumina.
- **Rectification:** Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
- **Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"):** Request the deletion of your personal data.
- **Restriction & Objection:** Object to or restrict the processing of your data.
- **Data Portability:** Request transfer of your data to another organization.
- **Withdraw Consent:** Withdraw any consent previously granted at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at `lumina@warclamerz.mfg`.
