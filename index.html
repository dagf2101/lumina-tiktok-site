Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 18, 2026 | Effective Date: September 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Lumina collects, uses, and safeguards personal information when interacting with our Service and the TikTok Developer Platform. We comply with GDPR, CCPA, and TikTok Developer Data Policies.

1. Information We Collect

  • TikTok Authentication Data: Open ID, Union ID, display name, and avatar URL provided via TikTok Login Kit.
  • Media & Publishing Tokens: Temporary OAuth tokens for video uploading and publishing, authorized by you.
  • News & Content Metadata: Titles, summaries, hashtags, and scheduling metadata.
  • Technical Logs: Server telemetry, IP addresses, and error metrics for security and rate limit tracking.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used exclusively to authenticate your identity, execute authorized publishing tasks, protect service integrity, and provide customer support. We never sell, rent, monetize, or broker personal data.

User Data Revocation & Deletion Instructions

To revoke access via TikTok:
1. Open TikTok app > Profile > Menu (top-right).
2. Tap Settings and privacy > Security and permissions > Manage app permissions.
3. Find Lumina and tap Remove access.

To request permanent deletion of your stored records:
Email lumina@warclamerz.mfg with subject "Data Deletion Request - Lumina TikTok" and your TikTok handle. We will permanently delete your records within 30 days.

3. Data Retention & Security

Tokens and processing files are held only as long as necessary to provide the service and are encrypted in transit (TLS/HTTPS) and at rest. We do not collect information from minors under 13.
